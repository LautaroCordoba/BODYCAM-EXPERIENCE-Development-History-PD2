# BODYCAM — Development History

This repository preserves the development history of **BODYCAM**, a mod for **PAYDAY 2** focused on creating a more immersive bodycam-style first-person experience.

It contains older versions of BODYCAM from different stages of its development, preserved to document how the mod evolved over time.

> **This is an archive of older development versions.**
>
> These builds are preserved for historical and development purposes and may contain unfinished features, experimental behavior, bugs, or systems that were later changed or replaced.

## About BODYCAM

BODYCAM is a PAYDAY 2 mod built around changing the feel of the first-person camera and weapon movement.

Throughout development, the project experimented with systems such as:

- Independent weapon movement
- FreeAim
- Camera and weapon inertia
- Weapon positioning
- Different movement profiles and presets
- First-person camera behavior
- Bodycam-inspired visual and gameplay presentation

The goal has been to create a different first-person experience while continuing to work within PAYDAY 2's existing systems.
